# Code of Conduct

We want Austial to be a friendly, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their experience level, background
or identity.

## Our standards

Expected:

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Disagree with ideas, not people. Assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to others.

Not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments, commits, issues and pull requests that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

Report problems privately through GitHub (a maintainer's profile, or a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/austial/austial-py/security/advisories/new) if you cannot reach
anyone else). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
